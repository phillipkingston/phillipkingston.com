Overview

The two podcast episodes are where the argument is made at length: what a large work model is on AWS for AI, and how far a real workflow can actually be automated on The Derby Mill Series. The keynote then shows the system running. The rest is 2024 on the road, where the same work met students, an AI community and a hackathon: a public lecture at the National Aviation University in Kyiv in March, and three short records from Abu Dhabi and Dubai in January and February. The two conferences at the foot of the page were not filmed, and are here as a record rather than something to watch. New talks are posted to the YouTube channel first and added here afterwards, where they play in place.

01

AWS for AI

56:36 Play “AppliedAI's world's first large work model”, AWS for AI episode 1, 56 minutes 36 seconds

AppliedAI's world's first large work model: predicting actions, not words

20 May 2025 Episode 1 56:36 Amazon Web Services

Kingston sets out Opus, the large work model behind AppliedAI. Where a language model predicts the next word, a work model predicts the next action: it draws on a knowledge graph of millions of documented business processes to generate a workflow and then optimise how that workflow runs.

The conversation goes from the architecture underneath Opus to what adoption looks like in regulated industries, how human and machine work are blended inside a single process, and what the shape of enterprise work becomes once this is ordinary rather than novel.

02

The Derby Mill Series

Play “Business Productivity”, The Derby Mill Series episode 15

Business productivity: what is the efficiency of an automated workflow at the limit?

31 Jul 2025 Episode 15 Intrepid Growth Partners

Arya Bolurfrushan and Phillip Kingston join the Derby Mill hosts on the mechanics of automating a real workflow — medical coding for hospitals among them — and on where human judgement and machine judgement each earn their place in one.

The question the episode is named for is the interesting one: if you keep optimising, what is the efficiency of a workflow at the limit, and what does a business look like once automation reaches every step rather than most of them?

GuestsArya Bolurfrushan, founder and CEO of AppliedAI, and Phillip Kingston, Member of Technical Staff.

HostsAjay Agrawal, Richard Sutton, Sendhil Mullainathan and Niamh Gavin, of Intrepid Growth Partners.

Play the public lecture “AI Case Usage in International Business”, National Aviation University, Kyiv

AI case usage in international business

26 Mar 2024 Kyiv, Ukraine Faculty of International Relations

A public lecture for students and lecturers of the National Aviation University in Kyiv, since renamed the National University “Kyiv Aviation Institute”, on where AI is already doing work in international business: systems that find and order the information a decision needs, what a chat model is and is not good for, and why a model that predicts can return an answer that is fluent, confident and wholly invented. Kingston also took the myths head on — the work is handed over under the control of the people who know it, not taken from them — and answered questions from the room on the skills that follow from that.

Conferences

Powering AI startups, at Step Conference

21 Feb 2024 Dubai Internet City Panel

Step is the startup conference of the MENA region, and its twelfth edition was the first to run an AI track of its own. AppliedAI sat with Microsoft and the Abu Dhabi Investment Office for “Powering AI startups”, a panel hosted by Microsoft for Startups UAE on what the region's AI companies can build locally and sell globally, the programmes behind them, and what the first years had taught everyone on the stage. It fell in the same fortnight in the Emirates as the hackathon above.
