Overview

Everything below is on the YouTube channel and plays here in place. The keynote is the substantive one: it walks through generating and executing workflows with Opus on Palantir's platform. The three clips that follow are from January and February 2024 in Abu Dhabi and Dubai, where the same work met students, an AI community and a hackathon.
